Our Story

A Coaching Centre Built for Assam's Students

Founded in 2015, Prime Institute began with just two classrooms and a simple belief — that quality education should be accessible to every student, in the language they understand best. Today we are a trusted Assamese medium coaching centre serving students from Class 6 to 12.

We specialise in the Arts and Commerce streams under SEBA (Class X) and AHSEC (Class XII). Alongside academics, we offer a fully equipped computer lab, guidance for science projects, and focused preparation for Olympiad and competitive exams.

  • Bilingual teaching with a strong Assamese-medium focus
  • Small batches for individual attention
  • Regular tests, assignments and doubt-clearing sessions
  • Digital literacy and computer education for all

Mission, Vision & Values

The principles that guide everything we do at Prime Institute.

Our Mission

To provide affordable, high-quality and concept-based education to every student of Assam in their own language, while nurturing curiosity, discipline and confidence.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted Assamese-medium coaching institute in Assam, known for academic excellence, digital literacy and holistic student development.

Our Values

Honesty, hard work, respect for every learner, equal opportunity, and a commitment to continuous improvement in everything we teach.
