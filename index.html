About Prime Institute

A Trusted Name for Arts & Commerce Coaching in Assam

Prime Institute is an Assamese medium coaching centre dedicated to students of Class 6 to 12. We prepare students for SEBA (Class X) and AHSEC (Class XII) examinations with a focus on concept clarity, regular practice and personal attention.

  • Assamese medium teaching with bilingual support
  • Regular tests, assignments and doubt-clearing classes
  • Computer education and digital literacy included
  • Guidance for science projects and Olympiad exams
